## 1. What this is

An interactive, cinematic, *fun* timeline of everything the United Nations has done on artificial intelligence — built for UN colleagues who range from "I run LLM evals for breakfast" to "is ChatGPT the robot from the movies?"

Three jobs, one site:

1. **A rich, trustworthy library.** Every milestone links to the original statement, resolution, or report. No hot takes without receipts.
2. **A translator.** Each event explained per audience: what it means for a diplomat, a political affairs officer, a techie, and a total newcomer.
3. **A portfolio piece.** It must make people say "wow" — proof of what one person + Claude Code can build, and proof that AI governance content doesn't have to be boring.

**The bar:** a First Committee delegate, a P-3 in DPPA, and a front-end developer should each spend 10 minutes on it and each come away having learned something — smiling.

## 3. The narrative structure — four zones

The site is a **fly-through galaxy**. You scroll (or click "engage thrusters") and travel through four zones. Each milestone is a glowing node; approach it and it expands.

### Zone 0 — Ancient History (1945 – Nov 2022)
One cheeky, nearly-empty stretch of space. A single dim star cluster with 3–4 tiny nodes and a caption like:

> *"For its first ~77 years, the UN was busy with minor side quests: peace, human rights, development. AI got the occasional polite mention."*

Include (tiny nodes, one-liners only):
- 1945 — UN Charter signed. Word count on "artificial intelligence": zero.
- 2017 — ITU launches the first **AI for Good** Summit; UNICRI opens its Centre for AI & Robotics. *("A few people saw it coming.")*
- 2020 — SG's **Roadmap for Digital Cooperation**.
- Nov 2021 — **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI** — the first global normative instrument on AI ethics. *("UNESCO called it before it was cool.")*

### Zone 1 — The Big Bang (30 Nov 2022)
ChatGPT launches. Visually: the star-field *ignites* — a supernova transition. Caption:

> *"On 30 November 2022, a chatbot dropped and suddenly every conference room in Turtle Bay had opinions about neural networks."*

### Zone 2 — The Main Galaxy (2023 → today)
Milestones organized as **three constellations** (color-coded, filterable):

**🏛️ Constellation Governance** (the "who's in charge here" cluster)
- Jul 2023 — First-ever **Security Council briefing on AI** (SG + experts).
- Oct 2023 — SG launches the **High-Level Advisory Body on AI** (39 experts).
- Dec 2023 — Advisory Body interim report, *Governing AI for Humanity*.
- Mar 2024 — **First GA resolution on AI** (A/78/L.49, US-led, adopted by consensus).
- Jul 2024 — Second GA resolution on **AI capacity-building** (China-led).
- Sep 2024 — Advisory Body **final report**; **Summit of the Future** adopts the **Global Digital Compact** (first truly universal AI-governance commitments).
- Aug 2025 — GA resolution establishing the **Independent International Scientific Panel on AI** and the **Global Dialogue on AI Governance**.
- Early 2026 — Scientific Panel members appointed *(Claude Code: verify exact date + names of co-chairs)*.
- **6–7 Jul 2026 — Inaugural Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva** — first time all UN Member States met in a dedicated AI-governance meeting; Scientific Panel presented its first annual report. Next session: **May 2027, New York**.

**🎪 Constellation Summits & Moments** (the "photo ops with robots" cluster)
- AI for Good Summits 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 (the 2023 robot press conference = comedy gold, use gently).
- UNIDIR Global Conferences on AI, Security & Ethics (latest: Jun 2026).
- SG's signature speeches/quotes per year — pull the best one-liners with links to full transcripts on UN WebTV / un.org.

**⚙️ Constellation Inside the Machine** (what the UN does *internally* with AI)
- **UN 2.0** and the Quintet of Change (data, digital, innovation, foresight, behavioural science).
- Secretariat guidance on staff use of generative AI; OICT initiatives *(Claude Code: research specifics — Unite tools, iSeek AI pilots, agency chatbots)*.
- Agency efforts worth a node: UNDP AI Hub, UNICEF AI for children, OCHA/anticipatory action, UNHCR, WFP, UNESCO implementation work. *(Research pass required — pick ~6 strongest with links.)*

### Zone 3 — The Horizon (what's next)
A short forward-looking zone: May 2027 Dialogue in NY, Scientific Panel's next reports, open questions. Ends with a call-to-action node: *"Want to be part of this? Start here →"* (links to learning resources, §6).

## 4. Tone rules — READ THIS TWICE, CLAUDE CODE

This will be public and attached to Mafi's professional profile. The humor must be:

✅ **Allowed:** self-deprecating nerd humor; gentle sarcasm about bureaucratic pace ("the UN discovered AI at the speed of a working group"); pop-culture analogies; jokes at the expense of *hype* or of Mafi himself; affectionate teasing of acronym soup.

❌ **Never:**
- Anything that makes the UN, its leadership, Member States, or specific officials look bad, incompetent, or hypocritical.
- Anything touching Member State politics, geopolitical rivalry framing (e.g., don't joke about "US vs China resolutions" — present both neutrally and positively).
- Anything that could be screenshot and used against Mafi or the Organization.
- Doom-y AI content presented as fact.

**Test for every joke:** *Could Mafi's future boss read this aloud in a town hall and everyone laughs — including the person joked about?* If not, cut it.

## 5. The content model — every milestone node

Each node opens a panel with **layered depth** (progressive disclosure — nobody is forced to read anything):

1. **The TL;DR** (2–3 sentences, fun voice). *"Mafi's take."*
2. **What actually happened** (5–8 factual sentences, neutral).
3. **"What's in it for me?" role toggle** — four tabs:
   - 🧑‍💼 **Diplomat / Member State delegate** — negotiation relevance, what your capital cares about.
   - 🕊️ **UN staffer (political/substantive)** — how it touches mandates, reporting, field work.
   - 🤓 **Techie** — the actual technical substance, what's real vs. ceremonial.
   - 🌱 **Complete newcomer** — plain-language version + "words you just met" mini-glossary.
4. **Receipts** — links to the original document, statement, resolution, UN WebTV recording, press release.
5. **Dig deeper** — optional hooks: a good YouTube explainer, a suggested prompt to paste into any AI chatbot ("Ask your favorite AI: *Explain the Global Digital Compact like I'm a first-year diplomat*"), a related node ("this connects to →").

## 6. BEEP-27 — the robot guide 🤖

**Working name: "BEEP-27"** (a UN-flavored droid: *Bureaucratic Explainer & Educational Protocol droid, model 27 — because everything at the UN has a resolution number*). Mafi may rechristen.

**Personality:** cheerful, slightly overwhelmed junior-staffer energy; loves acronyms a bit too much; explains everything through movies and shows.

**Behaviors:**
- Floats in a corner; click it anytime for context on the current node.
- **Analogy engine:** each jargon term gets a pop-culture analogy. Examples to seed:
  - *Asimov's Three Laws / I, Robot* → why "principles" aren't enough and you need governance ("Even in the movie, the rules had a loophole. That's why we write 60-page reports.")
  - *Star Wars* → the Scientific Panel = the Jedi Council of AI ("advisory, no lightsabers, sadly").
  - *Game of Thrones* → multilateral negotiation = getting all the houses to sign one document ("the Global Digital Compact is basically a peace treaty everyone actually signed").
  - *Rick and Morty* → model capabilities vs. alignment ("a portal gun is impressive; Rick using it responsibly is the hard part").
- **Glossary mode:** hover/tap any highlighted term (LLM, frontier model, interoperability, capacity-building…) → BEEP-27 explains in ≤2 sentences + one analogy.
- **"Teach me" mini-quests:** 3–5 optional guided paths ("The 10-minute diplomat's crash course", "AI jargon survival kit", "So you have to write a briefing note about AI").
- Fun facts sprinkled in ("The 2023 AI for Good summit held the world's first robot press conference. The robots were… diplomatic.").

**Scope discipline:** BEEP-27 is scripted content (JSON dialogue tree), *not* a live LLM — keeps the site static, fast, and free. But add one node where BEEP-27 says: "Want the real thing? Here's how to talk to an actual AI about this →" (links + suggested prompts).

## 7. Design direction

**Concept:** *"UN General Assembly hall meets deep space."* Not generic sci-fi — the palette and materials should quote the UN's own visual world.

**Palette (tokens):**
- `--void`: `#070B1A` (deep space navy — background)
- `--un-blue`: `#009EDB` (official UN blue — primary accent, node glow)
- `--assembly-gold`: `#C9A227` (the GA hall's gold emblem wall — highlights, era transitions)
- `--olive`: `#7A8B5C` (the olive branches — success/complete states, Zone 3 hope tones)
- `--paper`: `#F5F1E6` (aged resolution paper — text panels, so reading feels like documents, not terminals)
- `--signal`: `#FF6B4A` (sparingly — BEEP-27's personality color)

**Typography:**
- Display: something with diplomatic gravitas *and* a wink — e.g., a modern grotesque with character (Space Grotesk) for era titles.
- Body: highly readable humanist sans (e.g., Inter or Source Sans) — long-form panels must be comfortable.
- Utility/mono: for dates, resolution numbers, "receipts" (e.g., IBM Plex Mono) — resolution symbols like `A/RES/78/265` *deserve* monospace.

**Signature element (the one thing people remember):** the **Big Bang transition** — the moment scroll crosses 30 Nov 2022 and the dead star-field detonates into the full galaxy, with the UN emblem's olive branches briefly forming from particles. Spend the animation budget here; keep everything else disciplined.

**Motion rules:** GSAP ScrollTrigger for cinematic scroll; Three.js particle field reacts subtly to cursor/scroll velocity; `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a graceful static-but-beautiful vertical timeline fallback. Mobile gets a simplified (but still gorgeous) vertical mode.

## 8. Tech stack & architecture

- **Astro** (static output, content collections for milestones as Markdown/JSON — content lives separate from code, easy to update).
- **Three.js** — WebGL star-field/galaxy backdrop (own render layer, throttled, `IntersectionObserver`-gated).
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** — scroll choreography, node reveals, Big Bang sequence.
- **Vanilla/Astro islands** for the role-toggle panels and BEEP-27 (no React needed; keep JS lean).
- **Content schema** per milestone: `{ id, date, zone, constellation, title, tldr, factual, takes: {diplomat, staffer, techie, newcomer}, receipts: [{label, url}], digDeeper: [...], beepLines: [...], tags }`.
- **Deployment:** GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages. Repo public. README itself written in the site's voice (part of the portfolio flex).
- **Performance budget:** < 2.5s LCP on mid-range mobile; lazy-load Three.js; compress everything; Lighthouse ≥ 90 across the board.
- **Accessibility:** full keyboard navigation between nodes; ARIA on panels; captions/alt text; reduced-motion fallback is a first-class experience, not a punishment.
