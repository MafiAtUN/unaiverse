UN entities have piloted chatbots, virtual assistants, automated translation, satellite-image analysis, anomaly detection and decision-support tools. Public examples are available through UNHCR, WFP, WHO and UNDP. Documentation is uneven, and many pilots are described through innovation pages rather than formal impact evaluations.

**Sources:**  
https://www.unhcr.org/news/stories/explainer-how-unhcr-using-ai-support-refugees-and-improve-operations  
https://innovation.wfp.org/artificial-intelligence  
https://www.who.int/teams/digital-health-and-innovation/harnessing-artificial-intelligence-for-health  
https://www.undp.org/digital/ai

**Why it matters:** These pilots show how UN principles are being applied in services and operations, while exposing gaps in public evaluation.
