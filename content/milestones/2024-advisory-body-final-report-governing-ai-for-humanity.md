The Advisory Body released its final report after extensive consultations. It proposed seven measures: an international scientific panel on AI; an annual policy dialogue; an AI standards exchange; a capacity-development network; a global fund for AI; a global AI data framework; and a small AI office within the UN Secretariat. The report also called for global AI governance grounded in the UN Charter, international law, human rights and sustainable development.
