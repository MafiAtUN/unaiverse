The General Assembly adopted its first resolution devoted to lethal autonomous weapons systems by a recorded vote of 152 in favour, 4 against and 11 abstentions, at its fiftieth plenary meeting. The resolution requested the Secretary-General to seek views from States and relevant stakeholders and submit a report to the Assembly. It recognized the continuing work of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons Group of Governmental Experts while opening a universal General Assembly track.
