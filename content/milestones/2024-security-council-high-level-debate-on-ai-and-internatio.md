The United States convened a high-level Security Council briefing during its December presidency on artificial intelligence and the maintenance of international peace and security. It was the Council’s 9821st meeting. The discussion covered military applications, autonomous weapons, cyber risks, disinformation, strategic stability, international law and human control. The Secretary-General told the Council that AI was outpacing the world’s ability to govern it and urged Member States to agree international guard-rails.
