The General Assembly adopted without a vote the resolution establishing the Independent International Scientific Panel on Artificial Intelligence and the Global Dialogue on Artificial Intelligence Governance. The draft resolution was A/79/L.118, and the adoption meeting record was A/79/PV.89. The resolution provides detailed terms of reference and modalities, including a 40-member independent panel, geographical balance, annual scientific reporting and an inclusive annual dialogue. The Panel’s mandate is limited to the non-military domain.
