The Secretary-General launched a 39-member High-level Advisory Body on Artificial Intelligence for a one-year mandate. Its members served in their personal capacities and came from government, industry, academia and civil society across 33 countries. The Body was asked to advance recommendations on global AI governance, improve shared understanding of AI risks and opportunities, and support use of AI for the Sustainable Development Goals.
