ITU convened the 2023 summit in Geneva, focusing on generative AI, robotics, health, climate, accessibility and the Sustainable Development Goals. The programme combined demonstrations with policy and standards discussions. It marked the platform’s return to a major in-person summit after pandemic-era virtual activity.
