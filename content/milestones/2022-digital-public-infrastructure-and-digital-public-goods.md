UNDP and ODET support safe, inclusive and interoperable digital public infrastructure for identity, payments and data exchange. The Global Digital Compact encourages digital public goods and open-source technologies. ODET has also examined conditions under which AI systems may qualify as digital public goods.

**Sources:**  
https://www.undp.org/digital/digital-public-infrastructure  
https://www.un.org/digital-emerging-technologies/content/open-source-week-2025  
https://www.un.org/digital-emerging-technologies/sites/www.un.org.digital-emerging-technologies/files/bootstrap/cdn_providers/AI_Systems_as_Digital_Public_Goods.pdf

**Why it matters:** Open and interoperable infrastructure is a practical route for developing countries to access and shape AI-enabled public services.
