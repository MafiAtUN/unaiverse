OHCHR’s privacy-in-the-digital-age work increasingly addresses algorithmic decision-making, surveillance and artificial intelligence. OHCHR has emphasized that States must conduct human-rights due diligence when procuring, developing or using AI systems and must provide safeguards, transparency and access to remedy. This work is connected to recurring General Assembly and Human Rights Council resolutions on privacy in the digital age.

**Sources:**  
https://www.ohchr.org/en/privacy-in-the-digital-age  
https://www.ohchr.org/en/topic/digital-space-and-human-rights  
https://docs.un.org/en/A/HRC/48/31

**Why it matters:** It connects AI governance to established international obligations concerning privacy, equality, expression and due process.
