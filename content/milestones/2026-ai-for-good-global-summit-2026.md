The 2026 summit took place at Palexpo in Geneva from 7 to 10 July. It overlapped with the second day of the Global Dialogue but was a separately registered event. Its programme addressed standards, skills, policy, robotics and AI applications for global challenges.

**Sources:**  
https://aiforgood.itu.int/summit26/

**Why it matters:** The co-location created a Geneva cluster linking political dialogue, scientific assessment, technical standards and implementation.
