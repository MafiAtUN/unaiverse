The General Assembly adopted *Artificial intelligence in the military domain and its implications for international peace and security* by a recorded vote of 159 in favour, 2 against and 5 abstentions, at its fifty-fifth plenary meeting. The resolution addresses military AI beyond the narrower lethal autonomous weapons track. It requested the Secretary-General to seek views and prepare a report cataloguing opportunities, risks, challenges and existing or emerging normative proposals.

**Why it matters:** It created a broader General Assembly workstream on military AI, including decision support, command systems, cyber operations and strategic risk.
