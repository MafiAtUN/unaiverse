Heads of State and Government adopted the Pact for the Future at the Summit of the Future. The Global Digital Compact appears as Annex I and covers connectivity, digital inclusion, human rights online, data governance, digital public infrastructure, trust and safety, and AI governance. The Compact calls for establishment of an Independent International Scientific Panel on AI and a Global Dialogue on AI Governance. It also mandates a high-level review during the General Assembly’s eighty-second session.
