# IX. Key official quotations for website use

> “The alarm bells over the latest form of artificial intelligence — generative AI — are deafening, and they are loudest from the developers who designed it.”

**Event:** Secretary-General’s remarks to the Security Council, 18 July 2023.  
**Source:** https://press.un.org/en/2023/sgsm21880.doc.htm

> “We urgently need to incorporate the benefits of artificial intelligence into our lives, while protecting ourselves from its risks.”

**Event:** Secretary-General’s launch of the High-level Advisory Body on AI, 26 October 2023.  
**Source:** https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/press-encounter/2023-10-26/secretary-generals-remarks-press-conference-launching-the-high-level-advisory-body-artificial-intelligence

> “AI governance cannot be left to markets alone.”

**Event:** Secretary-General’s video message launching the Advisory Body’s final report, 19 September 2024.  
**Source:** https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statements/2024-09-19/secretary-generals-video-message-the-launch-of-the-final-report-of-the-high-level-advisory-body-artificial-intelligence-governing-ai-for-humanity

> “The establishment of the United Nations Independent International Scientific Panel on AI and the Global Dialogue on AI Governance marks a significant step forward in global efforts to harness the benefits of artificial intelligence while addressing its risks.”

**Event:** Statement by the Secretary-General welcoming the General Assembly’s adoption of A/RES/79/325, 26 August 2025.  
**Source:** https://press.un.org/en/2025/sgsm22776.doc.htm

**Why this section matters:** It provides short, transcript-linked statements suitable for timeline cards, headers or contextual callouts, subject to the quotation limits and attribution requirements of the final website.

**Standing rule (see `content/gaps.md` #17):** re-check every quotation against its linked transcript immediately before publication. Never quote from a press-release headline — headlines paraphrase and compress.
